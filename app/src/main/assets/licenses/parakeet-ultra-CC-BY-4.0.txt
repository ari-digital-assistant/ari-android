The European speech model is Parakeet Ultra by Moondream
(https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra), a further-trained version of
NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 (https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3).

Both are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Ari downloads the sherpa-onnx project's export of it
(https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet-tdt-0.6b-ultra-int8),
which is converted to ONNX and quantised to 8 bits. The model is not otherwise
changed. It is provided as-is, without warranties of any kind.
